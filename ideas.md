# Deespark Wellness — Design Exploration

## Three directions considered

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Chosen direction: Cobalt Botanical Ledger

### Design Movement

This direction draws from **contemporary editorial commerce** and **apothecary packaging systems**. It combines the supplied logo’s strong blue with the product photography’s natural greens and warmly lit neutrals to create a clear, professional wellness storefront.

### Core Principles

1. Present products as the source of visual proof: supplied imagery stays intact, large, and uncluttered.
2. Use editorial hierarchy instead of boxed template sections: oversized type, offset rule lines, and deliberate negative space create the navigation rhythm.
3. Build trust with practical information, direct WhatsApp ordering, and a restrained wellness disclaimer rather than exaggerated claims.
4. Keep every interaction fast, readable, and comfortably tappable on mobile.

### Color Philosophy

The visual field starts in warm paper white to let the varied supplied product photos retain their own colour accuracy. **Deespark Cobalt** anchors brand recognition and connects directly to the supplied logo, while leaf green appears in small botanical and status accents. Marigold is reserved for attentive moments such as selected tags and delivery cues, borrowing warmth from the product labels without overwhelming the interface.

### Layout Paradigm

The page follows a **vertical product journal** rather than a conventional centered card grid. A slim editorial masthead leads to an asymmetric hero: the copy sits in a left reading column, while the featured product image breaks the right edge. The catalogue then alternates image-forward product slabs, producing a rhythmic scroll that naturally adapts to a single-column mobile layout.

### Signature Elements

1. Cobalt vertical rules and numbered product markers create an ownable “ledger” motif.
2. Fine botanical linework and paper-grain texture frame content without competing with product photography.
3. Marigold capsule tags provide a visual shorthand for categories and ordering moments.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should reinforce clarity. Navigation expands into a simple sheet on mobile, product cards lift subtly without obscuring information, and WhatsApp CTAs provide immediate intent through a prefilled message. No interactions should mimic medical assessment or promise outcomes.

### Animation

Use a brief 180–240ms cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1) transition for hover, focus, and mobile-menu state changes. Sections may enter with a small upward translate and opacity transition only when motion is permitted. Buttons should scale to 0.97 on active press. Animations never move or manipulate the supplied product images beyond standard layout rendering.

### Typography System

**DM Serif Display** gives product names and editorial headlines a confident, humanist tone. **Manrope** carries navigation, metadata, body text, and CTA labels in a crisp, high-legibility sans serif. Headings use tight tracking at display scale; utility labels use uppercase, letter-spaced Manrope for quick scanning.

### Brand Essence

**Deespark Wellness makes product discovery and WhatsApp ordering for herbal wellness and eyewear feel clear, considered, and direct.**

Personality: **assured, grounded, attentive**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are concise and practical; CTAs describe the next clear action; microcopy avoids cures, hype, and filler.

> “Wellness choices, made easier to explore.”

> “Choose a product. Start your WhatsApp order.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The supplied blue Deespark mark is treated as the authoritative brand mark. It appears prominently in the masthead and is reused unchanged as the favicon.

### Signature Brand Color

**Deespark Cobalt — #1166D8**.

## Style Decisions

- Generous whitespace must remain visibly structured with ledger primitives such as cobalt rules, numbered markers, marigold tags, botanical annotation, or direct service cues.
- The supplied cobalt logo appears within a consistent **Deespark Wellness Company** lockup in the masthead and closing footer, while the icon alone remains appropriate for compact contexts such as the favicon.
- Every major section carries at least one ledger primitive so product, eyewear, service, contact, and ordering experiences read as one editorial-apothecary catalogue.
- The final ordering close must combine clear brand presence, direct WhatsApp reassurance, and the cobalt, green, and marigold system rather than relying on a single isolated CTA.
- Contact and service panels use the same ledger language as products: chapter indices, fine cobalt rules, warm paper fields, and restrained marigold annotations rather than generic boxed-card styling.
- The masthead lockup gives the unchanged supplied cobalt logo visible authority through an enlarged mark, a cobalt rule, and a deliberate Deespark Wellness Company wordmark treatment.
- Eyewear is framed as a numbered style-journal chapter with compact editorial metadata, cobalt rules, and marigold edition labels so it remains part of one Deespark catalogue.
